MM9 Native Builder: third-party code and tools

Included in this package
- Fallout-NV-MacOS (github.com/thetheoryofR/Fallout-NV-MacOS @ ff8e0d79), MIT licence,
  (c) 2026 thetheoryofR; see Fallout-NV-MacOS-LICENSE.txt. Copied: recomp/rt.h and the Ghidra
  scripts DumpPcode.java, FixEntries.java, FixNoReturn.java, ResolveJumpTables.java. Derived (changed):
  src/recomp/emit_rc.py and flaglive.py (x87 rounding-control fix, FFREE), and several files in
  src/host (each says so in its first lines).

Downloaded by the builder on your Mac (not included; each checked against a fixed SHA-256)
- Ghidra 12.1.4 (National Security Agency), Apache License 2.0. Runs on your Mac to analyse the game.
- Eclipse Temurin JDK 21.0.12.1 (Adoptium), GPLv2 with the Classpath Exception. Runs Ghidra.
- Unicorn 2.1.4 (the PyPI wheel's static library), GPLv2. Linked into the app the builder makes on
  your Mac, as the fallback interpreter for code the analysis missed. That app is never shared.

Optional, installed by you
- FFmpeg (for example: brew install ffmpeg). The app runs the ffmpeg command-line tool to play the
  game's Bink movies; nothing from FFmpeg is linked into the app.

Not included, and never shared: anything from Might and Magic IX. The builder reads your own copy
of the game and makes everything game-derived on your Mac.
